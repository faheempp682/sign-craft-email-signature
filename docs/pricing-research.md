## Observations for the owner

1. **Two distinct business models.** Enterprise tools (Exclaimer, Letsignit) charge a small amount per user per month, annual contract, and sell central deployment to the mail server or directory. Design-first tools (WiseStamp, MySignature) charge a flat or small per-seat fee for individuals and small teams. HubSpot gives the generator away as lead generation.
2. **The free generator is table stakes.** HubSpot offers an unlimited free generator with no account, and MySignature allows editing without one. Our free generator is on par; the differentiators would have to be template quality and being honest about mail-client support.
3. **Per-user prices are low ($0.90 to $2).** Revenue at that level depends on team size, and Exclaimer's 10-user minimum ($108 to $210 per year at its listed per-user rates) shows the floor the market uses. Revisit whether a per-seat model fits a tool that is internal-first.
4. **What the paid tiers actually sell:** central deployment and auto-updated details (the large-team value), campaigns and banners, analytics, and directory sync. Of those, this product has partial equivalents (campaigns and analytics are being built by other work streams); deployment depends on Gmail push being verified.
5. **Compliance badges matter to buyers.** Exclaimer lists multiple certifications. This product holds none, so public copy must not imply any.
6. **Verify before relying on these numbers.** Prices were read by an automated page reader, WiseStamp and Letsignit pages were not cross-checked against a second source, and MySignature's pricing could not be read at all. Open the five pages by hand before setting a price.
